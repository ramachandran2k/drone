Industries

Every mission is different. Every platform is too.

Stratosyn Origin works across seven operational sectors. In each one, we begin with the same question: what does the mission actually require, and what does physics say the platform must be able to do?

Defence & Homeland Security

The Challenge: Defence procurement cycles are long, operational requirements are classified, and many available systems were not designed for Indian terrain, Indian adversary threat profiles, or Indian regulatory frameworks.

What We Offer: Bespoke platform development under NDA, simulation validated against ballistic and hydrocode test data, counter-UAV flow and stability analysis, swarm architecture, and tethered ISR for persistent forward positioning.

Credibility Signal: UAV swarm demonstrations at an Indian Air Force station, national security deployments, and police pilot training across three states.

Surveillance & Reconnaissance

The Challenge: Standard commercial platforms lack the endurance, image quality, and operational reliability required for persistent intelligence missions in difficult environments.

What We Offer: Long-endurance fixed-wing and hybrid-VTOL platforms with integrated EO/IR payloads, stabilised gimbals, tethered platforms, and GCS mission planning for complex coverage.

Credibility Signal: One-hour surveillance quadcopter delivered to BHEL, 40-minute endurance police surveillance UAV for Tamil Nadu Police, and deployments for national disaster management departments.

Agricultural Automation

The Challenge: Commercial agricultural UAVs are imported, expensive to service, and rarely optimised for Indian crop geometries or field topologies.

What We Offer: Indigenous multirotor agricultural platforms with 10-15L spray capacity, CFD-optimised rotor downwash, agricultural component integration, and field-durable composite airframes.

Credibility Signal: Sanitizer spraying operations across Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh, agricultural integration expertise, and indigenous airframe manufacturing capability.

Industrial Inspection & Infrastructure

The Challenge: Manual inspection of pipelines, transmission lines, substations, and large industrial assets is expensive, slow, and hazardous. Consumer drone data is often insufficient for engineering-grade assessment.

What We Offer: Inspection-grade platforms with stabilised EO/IR sensors, lidar integration, precision positioning, industrial airframes, corridor mission planning, and data workflows suitable for engineering reporting.

Energy & Utilities

The Challenge: Energy companies need to monitor vast infrastructure networks with consistent data quality and regulatory compliance.

What We Offer: Long-endurance platforms for wide-area asset monitoring, tethered systems for continuous substation surveillance, sensor-to-data pipeline architecture, and physics-based EMI susceptibility studies.

Logistics & Last-Mile Delivery

The Challenge: Aerial delivery requires reliability, safety, compliance, payload drop precision, urban obstacle awareness, and weather robustness.

What We Offer: Payload dropping UAV development, endurance and range optimisation through CFD, structural validation of payload bay mechanics, and regulatory pathway advisory support.

Research & Advanced Aerospace Programs

The Challenge: Academic and government research programs need flight-test vehicles faster than industry can deliver them, with the technical documentation depth that research requires.

What We Offer: Rapid experimental platform development, blended-wing UAV design and test, autonomous weather monitoring systems, high-speed aerodynamic simulation, and co-development programs.

Credibility Signal: NREL autonomous weather monitoring copter program at SRM University, blended-wing UAV construction and testing, and RC-to-full-scale transition methodology.
