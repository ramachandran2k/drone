Programs

Not every program fits a catalogue. Stratosyn Origin was built for the ones that do not.

Confidential engineering partnerships for bespoke UAV systems.

We work directly with defence establishments, industrial operators, research institutions, and technology companies to deliver bespoke UAV systems and long-term engineering partnerships. Every engagement starts with a Mission Definition phase because we will not begin engineering until we understand the mission at the level the mission demands.

Programs are conducted under NDA as standard. Technical data and program details are handled on secure, access-controlled systems. We do not discuss one client's program with another.

Turnkey Platform Development

Complete platform design, simulation, fabrication, integration, and flight validation for a specific use case. Delivered as a fully tested, documented, deployment-ready system.

Suitable for: defence RFQ responses, industrial platform procurement, research vehicle development.

Collaborative R&D Program

Joint development with a client's engineering team, DRDO laboratory, or private OEM to co-develop platform capability, simulation toolchains, or indigenous solver modules.

Suitable for: defence R&D, academic research programs, iDEX / DISC challenge responses.

Long-Term Innovation Partnership

Multi-year framework agreements providing continuous simulation support, iterative platform development, and sustained engineering capability to evolving product lines or operational fleets.

Suitable for: defence OEMs, industrial multi-site deployments, and government aerial surveillance infrastructure.

Program Process

Four phases from mission to deployment.

Mission Definition

Define the use case, payload requirements, operational environment, performance constraints, and target mission parameters.

Output: a signed technical brief and NDA.

Engineering Sprint

Parallel simulation and conceptual design tracks, with CFD/FEA models running while structure and propulsion options are defined.

Fast cycle. Data-driven decisions. Iterative review every two weeks.

Prototype Development

Physical build begins from validated simulation outputs, including composite fabrication, propulsion build, avionics integration, and payload fit-check.

First flight is scheduled within the agreed program plan.

Validation & Deployment

Structured ground and flight test program, documentation package for design audit, certification, or technology transfer, plus post-delivery support and extension options.

Program Standards

Built for confidential, auditable delivery.

  • All programs conducted under NDA
  • Computation performed on secure, access-controlled systems
  • Simulation models delivered with validation documentation
  • Physical platforms delivered with test evidence packages
  • Suitable for design audit, certification, and technology-transfer dossiers
  • iDEX / DISC / Make-in-India program compatible
