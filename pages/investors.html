India's End-to-End Autonomous Aerial Systems Company.

We are building the engineering infrastructure India needs to develop, validate, and deploy advanced unmanned aerial systems indigenously at scale.

The opportunity.

India's defence UAV market is projected to exceed USD 4 billion by 2030. The country currently imports the majority of its advanced UAV technology. The government's Aatmanirbhar Bharat initiative, iDEX program, and Make-in-India defence policy are creating unprecedented procurement pathways for indigenous developers, but very few companies have the technical depth to credibly compete at the systems level.

Stratosyn Origin is positioned to be one of them.

Why Now

  • India's defence procurement framework is prioritising indigenous suppliers.
  • iDEX / DISC funding is available for startups solving defined defence technology gaps.
  • The global counter-UAV market is growing rapidly, and anti-drone simulation is a capability we already possess.
  • Commercial UAV markets in agriculture, industrial inspection, and logistics are scaling as regulation matures.
  • India has deep aerospace talent but very few vertically integrated UAV development companies.

What We Are Looking For

We are seeking seed to pre-Series A capital to accelerate platform development programs, expand manufacturing capability, and build the simulation infrastructure that will underpin our digital twin offering at scale.

We are looking for investors who understand defence-tech timelines, have patience for program-based revenue cycles, and share our conviction that India's autonomous aerial future must be built in India.
