About Stratosyn Origin

Twenty Years of Hard Engineering. One Company to Show for It.

Stratosyn Origin did not start in a boardroom. It started at an airfield, in a simulation lab, in a composite layup bay, and inside the defence procurement offices of India's armed forces.

Built by Engineers Who Have Operated at the Edge.

Our founders have designed and flown UAVs that broke world records. They have built hybrid VTOL systems, IC-engine long-endurance platforms, and tethered aerial surveillance systems deployed by state police forces, disaster management agencies, and the Indian Air Force. The team includes international FAI UAV pilot credentials and over 1,800 flight hours across fixed-wing, multirotor, hybrid, and VTOL platforms.

Others on the founding team have designed aircraft interiors at Airbus, run digital product portfolios for aerospace SaaS platforms, managed defence pre-sales programs exceeding USD 1.3 billion in value, and earned advanced degrees from Cranfield University. They have sat in the simulation lab running CFD models validated against hydrocode data and live fired-test results, and they have sat across the table from procurement officers in defence ministries.

Together, that combination of composite-layup experience, simulation depth, and defence-market fluency is what Stratosyn Origin is. Not a software company that decided to build drones. Not a drone company that added a simulation tab to its website. A full-stack aerospace engineering organisation built to make systems fly the way they were designed to fly.

Vision

To become India's most trusted indigenous developer of advanced unmanned aerial systems, building platforms that are engineered in simulation, validated in flight, and deployed in mission-critical environments across defence, security, and industry.

Mission

To close the gap between India's defence and industrial requirements and the systems that can fulfil them through physics-based engineering, precision manufacturing, and end-to-end program delivery that replaces imported capability with indigenous excellence.

Why Stratosyn Origin

Five pillars behind the company.

Simulation Before Metal

We run thousands of virtual flight hours before the first prototype is built. Our CFD and FEA models are validated against real-world test data, not approximations, compressing the development timeline.

End-to-End, No Subcontracting

From the first concept sketch to final flight validation, everything is done in-house. No design-build split, no outsourced simulation, one team, one accountability chain, one outcome.

Defence-Native Understanding

We speak the language of procurement, RFQ responses, NDA structures, classified program management, and mission-specific customisation. You do not need to translate your requirement into engineering language.

Aatmanirbhar at the Core

India's autonomous aerial capability should not depend on foreign supply chains or imported IP. Every platform we build is a step toward that independence.

Proven at the Limit

World records, international competition wins, Air Force deployments, and simulation work matched against hydrocode data. We have operated at the edge of what this technology can do.

Mission-ready systems

The next generation of Indian aerial systems starts here.
